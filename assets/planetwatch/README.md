# Planetwatch close-up photos

Drop a landscape image in this folder named by the landmark's **key** and the app
will use it automatically — as the close-up backdrop and in the 📷 "real photo"
lightbox. Any landmark without an image here keeps its procedural 3D scene.

**Filename = `<key>.jpg`** (one of):

reef, everest, canyon, richat, baikal, nile, amazon, bahamas, uyuni, betsiboka,
greenland, sahara, palm, pyramids, almeria, pivot, korea, greatwall, hurricane,
aurora, volcano, lightning

Example: the Great Barrier Reef close-up uses `reef.jpg`.

## Recommended specs
- Format: JPG or WebP (WebP is smaller)
- Aspect: landscape 16:9
- Size: ~1600–1920px wide, under ~350 KB
- No text, watermark, or borders — full-bleed image
- Use AI-generated (e.g. ChatGPT) or images you have the rights to — the site is public.
